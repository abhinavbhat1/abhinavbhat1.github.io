Research Accepted poster · AMIA 2026 2025 – present

PACD: a contextual-bandit system for medication adherence in cardiovascular disease

Research Collaborator with Dr. Yuri Quintana, Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center / Harvard Medical School
  • Designed PACD, a contextual-bandit framework that chooses which adherence intervention to offer each patient, learning from outcomes over time.
  • The policy only ever picks from a clinician-approved action set, so learning never overrides clinical judgement.
  • Accepted as a poster at the AMIA 2026 Annual Symposium.
Try it · Contextual banditPick a patient profile, tick which options the clinician allows, and press play. The policy learns which option keeps that kind of patient on their medication. Simulated patients, not clinical data.
