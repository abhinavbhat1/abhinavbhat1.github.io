Predicting post-contrast 3D breast MRI from pre-contrast scans

Research Assistant, Sumit Chopra Lab at NYU Langone Health. 2026 to present.

  • Self-supervised LeJEPA representations of 3D MRI, learning to predict contrast enhancement so that fewer scans need a gadolinium injection.

Hide part of a scan, predict the hidden part from what is visible.

LUMINA Wearables: the archive layer of an AI-ready patient record

Research Collaborator, DCI Network at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center and Harvard Medical School, with HealthKey. 2025 to present.

  • Own work package 3 of LUMINA, the DCI Network's reference architecture for a longitudinal, multimodal patient record: a raw-readings archive, device sync and daily derivation into OMOP, delivered as pull requests to PRomop, HealthKey's open-source patient record.
  • Found five concept-mapping issues for the OMOP work package, including the heart-rate-variability mix-up (SDNN against RMSSD) that became my semantic-drift research question.

Why the archive needs a daily derivation step: raw wearable data is enormous.

PACD: a contextual-bandit system for medication adherence

Research Collaborator with Dr. Yuri Quintana at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center and Harvard Medical School. 2025 to present.

  • Personalises cardiovascular adherence interventions while only ever choosing from a clinician-approved set.
  • Presenting at the AMIA 2026 Annual Symposium in Dallas on 9 November.

Tick what the clinician allows, then let the system learn which option keeps patients on their medication.
